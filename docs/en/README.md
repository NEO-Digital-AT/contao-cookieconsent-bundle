# NEO Cookie Consent – documentation

NEO Cookie Consent shows a cookie banner on every page of a Contao website and
holds back services of other providers until the visitor consents. The services
are kept in the back end; templates for 47 common services fill in provider,
purpose, cookies and the privacy link. A daily scan reads the pages, adds the
services it recognises and reports unknown addresses.

The extension is a tool. Whether a website meets the legal requirements is the
responsibility of its operator.
